## About Me

I am a **Robotics & Intelligent Systems undergraduate and Student Researcher at Bahria University**, working at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Robotics, Generative AI and Embedded Systems**.

My primary interest is in developing intelligent systems capable of moving beyond conventional software toward systems that can **perceive their environment, reason over information, make decisions and interact with the physical world**.

My work spans **machine learning, explainable AI, retrieval-augmented generation, natural language processing, autonomous navigation, embedded intelligence and predictive modeling**, with applications across robotics, financial intelligence, technical assessment and economic forecasting.
